Eurostat dissemination API (JSON-stat), queried 2026-11-15 by the editor's agent. Datasets and filters: ilc_li09 (at-risk-of-poverty rate before all social transfers, pensions excluded from income), ilc_li10 (before social transfers except pensions, so pensions count as income), ilc_li02 (after social transfers). Filters for all three: geo=EU27_2020 and BE, sex=T, age=TOTAL, threshold 60% of median equivalised income (B_60), unit=PC (% of population), time 2024 and 2025. Base URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/<dataset> . Survey year 2025 refers to incomes of 2024.
